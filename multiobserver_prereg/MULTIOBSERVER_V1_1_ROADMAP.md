# CCSU Multiobserver v1.1 — Scientific Roadmap

**Status:** canonical working order  
**Principle:** no programme advances merely because the previous one produced positive effects; each transition requires methodological gates.

## Stage 1 — CCSU-MO-NS-EOS-001

**Niche:** neutron-star equation of state through nuclear multimessenger astronomy.

Purpose:

- validate domain-conditioned public coherence with known synthetic truth;
- test partial TOV, tidal, mass–radius, and low-density translations;
- verify that the Atlas improves held-out prediction without replacing local EOS charts;
- exercise minority-evidence, provenance, robustness, and the recovered P5 control.

Transition gate to Stage 2:

1. P5 passes before substantive interpretation;
2. P1–P4 decisions are produced without deviations or hidden threshold changes;
3. local-model diversity remains above its frozen retention gate;
4. negative controls show that prior harmonization does not manufacture coherence;
5. an independent physics review accepts the TOV/Love implementation and synthetic truth generator.

Failure response: repair or revise NS-EOS in a newly registered version. Do not use Hubble inference as a substitute replication.

## Stage 2 — CCSU-MO-H0-001

**Niche:** multi-probe inference of the Hubble constant and distance–redshift relations.

Purpose:

- translate CMB, distance-ladder, BAO, and standard-siren relations without treating their \(H_0\) estimates as exchangeable measurements;
- distinguish calibration conflict from cosmological-model dependence;
- test whether the Atlas improves cross-probe prediction while retaining conditional local models.

Additional gates:

1. sound-horizon and distance-ladder calibration assumptions remain explicit public dependencies;
2. posterior samples are never pooled before likelihood/prior separation;
3. ΛCDM and preregistered extensions are separate local model families;
4. synthetic tension and no-tension controls recover their generating class at calibrated error rates;
5. results remain stable under leave-one-probe-out analysis.

Transition gate to Stage 3: Stage 2 must demonstrate that relational coherence can be distinguished from averaging away a real scientific tension.

## Stage 3 — CCSU-MO-QRF-001

**Niche:** quantum reference frames in finite-group quantum circuits.

Purpose:

- test exact frame translations, cycle holonomy, and invariant operational probabilities;
- determine whether CCSU adds explanatory or diagnostic value beyond a restatement of quantum-reference-frame covariance;
- extend the Atlas from observational model plurality to observer-dependent subsystem descriptions.

Entry conditions:

1. Stages 1 and 2 have validated the Atlas under both known-truth and real-tension conditions;
2. the QRF protocol specifies operational invariants before choosing an interpretation of quantum mechanics;
3. finite Abelian circuits pass exact compilation-oracle tests;
4. Wigner-friend scenarios, if included, are stress tests rather than the primary domain.

Success criterion: CCSU must detect invalid translations or holonomy while preserving frame-dependent local descriptions. Formal consistency alone is insufficient.

## Programme-level stop rule

The roadmap pauses if any stage:

- requires a metaobserver with privileged access unavailable to the scientific problem;
- collapses local models into a single ontology to obtain a positive result;
- cannot separate translation error from substantive scientific disagreement;
- fails its positive control;
- changes a primary rule after inspecting registered outcomes.

No global score is accumulated across stages. Each niche receives an independent protocol, freeze, execution, and interpretation.
