  \underbrace{V_{24}}_{\text{24-cell vertices}}\times
  \underbrace{\{f,\chi,c,\sigma\}}_{\text{type fiber}}
\]
the \emph{semantic lattice}, partially ordered by reachability along legal
collapse moves (energy‐monotone steps) and triality lifts. Here
\(V_{24}\subset D_4\subset \Lambda_{E_8}\) are the \(24\) vertices of the
\(D_4\)–24-cell sitting in the \(E_8\) weight lattice. The type fiber encodes
SM chiral content; filtering the fiber to \(\nu_L\) (left neutrinos only)

\paragraph{Triality action.}

with \(\sigma\) unchanged and \((f',\chi',c')\) permuted consistently with the

\section{Layer Invariants}
\label{sec:layer-invariants}

Layer separation \( \mathcal E \to \mathcal I \to \mathcal M \) is only meaningful if
certain quantities are preserved or monotone. We state the three invariants we
enforce in code and in the acceptance tests A1–A4.

\paragraph{Energy layer (\(\mathcal E\)).}
For neutral windows (after \(\Pi\)), the Lyapunov functional
\[
  E[\psi] \;=\; \int\!\Big(\tfrac{\beta}{2}|\nabla\psi|^2 + \tfrac{\alpha}{4}|\psi|^4\Big)\,d^3x
\]
is nonincreasing along~\eqref{eq:neutral-pde}. Under bounded observer pulses,
\(\Pi/\Lambda\) toggles perturb \(E\) only at \(O(\|A_{\rm obs}\|^2)\) margins (A1–A2).

\paragraph{Information layer (\(\mathcal I\)).}
Mixing information (CKM/PMNS) is summarized by the Jarlskog invariants
\(J_q,J_\ell\). Our \(\Lambda\)–mode warps preserve them up to a tolerance
\(\varepsilon_J\) (by design in the scaffold):
\[
  |J_{\rm after}-J_{\rm before}| \le \varepsilon_J ,
  \qquad
  \varepsilon_J \approx 10^{-3}\ \text{(quarks)},\quad
  \varepsilon_J \approx 10^{-2}\ \text{(leptons)}.
\]
Angles may move (predictions §\ref{chap:predictions}), but \(J\) remains within band.

\paragraph{Meaning layer (\(\mathcal M\)).}
Counts of semantic identities are invariant under legal moves:
(i) \(\Pi/\Lambda\) toggles, (ii) triality lifts \(\mathsf T\), and (iii) collapse
on a fixed shell do not change the number of distinct \(\mu\) realized from a
given fiber filter. In particular: \emph{(a)} the “192” superset is stable,
\emph{(b)} the minimal neutrino filter produces 24 per flavor, and
\emph{(c)} generation-lock preserves three connected shells.
