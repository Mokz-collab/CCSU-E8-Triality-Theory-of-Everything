\section{Identity Gradient Protocol}
\[
G(\psi)=-\frac{d}{dt}\|\psi-\psi_a\|_2^2.
\]
\textbf{Admit} if $G>0$ with margin on $\Delta t$; \textbf{hold} if near zero; \textbf{dissolve} otherwise.
